03 / A SHARED FOUNDATION

One account.
Both products.

MeepSheep is being built around one shared identity, so a person who joins through SynTalk or SYNEAR can access both products without creating a second account.

That shared sign-in does not collapse the products together. Each service keeps its own tools, data, and authorization boundaries.
